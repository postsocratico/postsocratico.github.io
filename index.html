Welcome Data Driven Governance

"Complex computation and network analysis can help redefine the path of economic development and urban planning."

Degree Centrality

Quantifies the total number of direct connections a specific entity or region holds within the broader network.

Practical Application

Identify primary economic hubs or highly integrated urban zones. Regions with high degree centrality are prime targets for direct capital injection to maximize immediate local impact.

Betweenness Centrality

Measures how frequently a node acts as the shortest bridging path between other disconnected nodes in the system.

Practical Application

Pinpoint critical infrastructure or supply chain bottlenecks. Investing in high-betweenness regions stabilizes the broader economy by fortifying the bridges between disparate markets.

Community Detection

Algorithmic grouping of nodes into densely interconnected clusters, revealing the natural structural subdivisions of a network.

Practical Application

Discover organic economic zones that transcend traditional administrative borders. Enables policymakers to design regional strategies based on actual trade and mobility flows rather than arbitrary lines.
