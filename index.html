Miguel [Apellido]

Economics undergraduate at UNAM. I work on input-output networks, economic complexity, and green finance — and spend the rest of my time on classical guitar and writing.

I'm a research assistant working primarily with Dr. Clemente Ruiz Durán, with some collaboration involving Dr. Michael Piore at MIT. My empirical work sits at the intersection of network theory and development economics: input-output structure, centrality, global value chains, and the financing of the energy transition. Most of it is built in R.

Outside of that, I play classical guitar — currently working through Bach's lute suites — and write, mostly in Spanish, on film, music, and ideas at postsocratico.com.
